Restaurant consulting for kitchen exhaust

With over 50 years of combined restaurant and code experience, Ecoclean consultants help restaurants design, build and run kitchen exhaust systems that meet NFPA 96.

  • Design. Work with your design team on NFPA 96 and local code, and on a system you can keep running well.
  • Construction. Catch design and installation problems before they become a fire hazard and an expensive repair.
  • Operations. On-site training for managers and kitchen staff on safe operation and maintenance.
